A Quick Look at Tim Hortons

Tim Hortons was founded in 1964 by Canadian hockey legend Tim Horton in Hamilton, Ontario. What started as a simple coffee and donut shop has grown into one of the largest and most recognizable quick-service restaurant chains in North America, with thousands of locations across Canada, the United States, and beyond.

The brand has always stood for one thing: honest food and drink at a fair price. That reputation is a huge reason why Tim Hortons menu prices remain a top search for millions of customers every month.

Tim Hortons Value Deals & How to Save

Tim Hortons regularly offers promotions that are worth watching for. Here are a few reliable ways to save:

2 for $6 Menu — A popular deal offering two select items (usually a drink and a food item) for $6.00.

Tims Rewards App — Tim Hortons runs a loyalty program through its app where frequent customers earn points toward free drinks and food. It's one of the easiest ways to cut down your spending if you visit regularly.

Combo Meals — Ordering a combo (sandwich + drink + hash browns) almost always works out cheaper than buying the items separately.

Seasonal Promotions — The brand regularly runs time-limited deals around holidays and new product launches. Following Tim Hortons on social media or checking the app is the best way to stay up to date.

Tim Hortons Menu Prices: USA vs. Canada

One thing worth knowing is that Tim Hortons menu prices differ between the US and Canada. Canadian prices are in Canadian dollars, while US prices are in USD. Beyond the currency difference, the menus themselves are slightly different — Canada tends to have more options, including the Loaded Bowls and certain regional specialties.

In Canada, most individual menu items fall in the $2–$5 CAD range, which makes Tim Hortons one of the most affordable daily coffee and food options in the country.

FAQs - Tim Hortons Menu Prices

  1. What is the cheapest item on the Tim Hortons menu?
    A: A single shot of espresso starts at around $1.49, making it one of the most affordable options. A plain donut can also be had for as little as $1.00 at many locations.
  2. How much does a medium coffee cost at Tim Hortons?
    A: A medium brewed coffee at Tim Hortons is typically around $2.09 in the US, though prices can vary slightly by location.
  3. Does Tim Hortons have a value menu?
    A: Yes. Tim Hortons offers a "2 for $6" menu deal at many locations, and in 2026 they introduced a buy-any-beverage promotion where you can add a donut for $1, a bagel with cream cheese for $2, or a breakfast sandwich for $3.
  4. Are Tim Hortons prices the same everywhere?
    A: No. Franchise owners set their own prices within certain guidelines, so Tim Hortons menu prices can vary by city, state, or province. Prices in major cities tend to be slightly higher than in smaller towns.
  5. Does Tim Hortons offer a loyalty or rewards program?
    A: Yes. The Tims Rewards program lets you earn points on every purchase through the app, which can be redeemed for free food and beverages.
  6. What is the most popular item at Tim Hortons?
    A: The Double-Double — a coffee with two creams and two sugars — is arguably the most iconic and beloved item on the Tim Hortons menu, particularly in Canada.
  7. Are Tim Hortons prices higher than Starbucks or Dunkin'?
    A: Generally, Tim Hortons menu prices are more affordable than Starbucks and roughly comparable to Dunkin', especially for standard coffee and breakfast items.
