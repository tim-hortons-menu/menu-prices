A Quick Look at Tim Hortons

Tim Hortons was founded in 1964 by Canadian hockey legend Tim Horton in Hamilton, Ontario. What started as a simple coffee and donut shop has grown into one of the largest and most recognizable quick-service restaurant chains in North America, with thousands of locations across Canada, the United States, and beyond.

The brand has always stood for one thing: honest food and drink at a fair price. That reputation is a huge reason why Tim Hortons menu prices remain a top search for millions of customers every month.
